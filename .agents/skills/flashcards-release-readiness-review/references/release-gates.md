# Release gates

## Product

- V1 scope is complete or explicitly deferred.
- Create, edit, learn, sync, publish, moderate, export, and delete paths pass.

## Integrity

- Scheduler fixtures, sync idempotency, conflict, migration, backup, and restore tests pass.

## Security and legal

- Authorization, content security, dependency, privacy, DSA, minors, and accessibility reviews have no blockers.
- Operator, hosting, contacts, retention, and store declarations are final.

## Operations

- Monitoring, alerting, support, moderation ownership, rollback, staged rollout, and incident response are ready.

## Distribution

- Web production build, Android App Bundle, and iOS archive are signed from the reviewed commit.
