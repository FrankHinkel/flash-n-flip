The checked-in MP3 files are derived from the source archive by
`scripts/generate-music-soundfont.mjs`. The script maps the SFZ region and
`pitch_keycenter` declarations to MIDI notes 21 through 108, resamples to
44.1 kHz mono, applies a peak limiter and encodes at 64 kbit/s. The generated
manifest records the size and SHA-256 digest of every derived file.
