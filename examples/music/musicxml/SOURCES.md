# MusicXML reference corpus

These files exercise the offline `mxml2abc` curation tool. They are not bundled
into the app or curated catalog. Hashes pin the exact files used by regression
tests.

`beethoven-fuer-elise.musicxml` identifies itself as `OpenScore (CC0)`. The
other nine files come from the `sevagh/musicxml-library` collection, whose
README describes its MuseTrainer corpus as public-domain MusicXML. Most of
those individual files do not contain a concrete license statement. Treat that
collection-level assertion as provenance, not as a final legal clearance for
redistribution in an FnF deck; verify the concrete edition before publishing.
